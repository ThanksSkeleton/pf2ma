# PF2E Monster Archetype Bank

Browse 31 reusable PF2E creature archetypes using the carousel. Choose a level from −1 through 24, a physical damage type, and a nonphysical damage type to generate a stat block, tactics, and abilities. Engineer includes its attached minion stat blocks. Fixed damage types retain their listed type.

Flavor each creature for your encounter without tying its mechanics to a particular story or appearance.
