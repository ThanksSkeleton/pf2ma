# PF2E Monster Archetype Bank

Browse 31 reusable PF2E creature archetypes using the carousel. Choose a level from −1 through 24 and a damage type to generate a stat block, tactics, and abilities. Engineer includes its attached minion stat blocks. Fixed ability damage types, such as fire, retain their listed type.

Flavor each creature for your encounter without tying its mechanics to a particular story or appearance.
